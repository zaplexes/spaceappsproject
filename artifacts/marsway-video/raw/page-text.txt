DATA & PROVENANCE
Know what you’re looking at.

Public imagery via NASA Trek and Caltech/Esri, global relief and geological context, plus local Jezero elevation and estimated slope. Feature-value suggestions use editable simulation preferences; terrain-aware routing and 3D relief remain unavailable.

Orbital images show regional context. Rover images show local surface observations. Elevation and slope colours are derived from a DEM; geological units are mapped interpretations. Direct mineral measurements remain unavailable. The IAU/USGS feature catalogue provides names and discovery centres; it does not supply verified observation sites. Global colour hillshade provides relief context without numeric heights.

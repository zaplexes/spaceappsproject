## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished original logo for MARTIAN MAP, a source-linked Mars exploration and science trip-planning app. Transparent background, horizontal logo lockup with a compact distinctive symbol at left and the exact words "MARTIAN MAP" at right. Symbol: a rust-red Martian disk with three bold flowing topographic contour cuts; a simple route terminating in one small destination point subtly integrated into its negative space. Prioritize a memorable simple silhouette and legibility at small app-header size. Typography: refined modern geometric sans serif uppercase, MARTIAN semibold and MAP lighter, carefully spaced. Palette: rust/terracotta #B4472D for the mark and deep forest-charcoal #21332C for text, suited to the app's warm ivory interface. Flat vector-like graphic treatment with crisp clean edges. Generous clear space around the whole lockup. No gradients, shadows, mockups, extra text, NASA insignia, rocket clipart, or watermark.
